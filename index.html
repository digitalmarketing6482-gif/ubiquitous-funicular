How do I join Ruin Roleplay II?

To join Ruin Roleplay II, click on the "WHITELIST NOW" button, join our Discord server, and fill out the quick whitelist application form. Once approved, you can connect directly through FiveM.

What are the server specifications?

Our server runs on dedicated high-performance hardware with AMD Ryzen processors, enterprise SSD storage, and top-tier DDoS protection, ensuring a lag-free 60+ FPS experience for all players.

Is there a roleplay guide for beginners?

Yes! We have a complete Server Book available on our Discord server that outlines all rules, basic roleplay terms (like Powergaming, Metagaming, Fail-RP), and tips to help you get started on your journey.

How can I contact support or report a player?

You can open a support ticket on our Discord server 24/7. Our dedicated staff team is always ready to assist you with player reports, bug submissions, or general inquiries.
